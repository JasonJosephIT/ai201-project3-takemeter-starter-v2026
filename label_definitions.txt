analysis: takes a position, and a specific checkable fact (number, date, quote, record, named comparison) is a reason for it
hot_take: takes a confident position about the world with no load-bearing support; any number is decoration
reaction: takes no position; the writer responds to something they are seeing or just experienced, in the moment
explainer: takes no position and is not about the writer's own experience; asks for or passes on information (question, recommendation request, how-to, news)
anecdote: takes no position; the writer tells a story about something they experienced that is already over
advertisement: promotes a business, product, service, event or giveaway the writer is connected to and invites readers to buy, visit, sign up or attend; a customer recommending a place is not an ad
